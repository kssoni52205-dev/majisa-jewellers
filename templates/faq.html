Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about shopping with Majisa Jewellers.

How can I place an order?
Browse our collection, open the product you like, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details and complete the order.
Do I need an account to place an order?
You can browse products without an account. If the checkout flow requires an account, you can quickly create one using the registration page.
How can I check my order?
After logging in, open the My Orders section. Your placed orders and their current status will be displayed there.
Can I add products to my wishlist?
Yes. Use the wishlist option on products you want to save for later. Your saved products can be viewed from the Wishlist page.
How can I contact Majisa Jewellers?
You can contact us through our Contact Us page, call us at 8949144970, or message us through our Instagram account.
Where can I find my order details?
Go to My Orders and select the order you want to view. The order details page shows the items, payment information, delivery address and total.
What payment methods are available?
Available payment options are shown during checkout. The exact options depend on the payment methods configured for the store.
How can I get help with an existing order?
Contact our team with your order number and the issue you are facing. We will help you with the next steps.
